What Muster does
Muster (app.muster) is a nearby group communication app. It exchanges content directly between phones over Bluetooth, including carrying updates and encrypted private-message envelopes through other members. The app has no INTERNET permission, developer server, cloud account, advertising, analytics, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Offline does not mean your content stays only on your phone.

Information on your phone and other members' phones
Muster stores a generated cryptographic installation identity, group invitations and keys, display names, optional profile descriptions/pictures, posts, comments, reactions, polls/votes, private messages, shared-file listings/payloads, moderation actions, reporting messages, sync records, preferences and diagnostic counts. Group content and profiles are available to members receiving them. Private-message bodies use separate sender/recipient encryption; relay members can still observe endpoints, timing and sizes. Moderation reports go to the moderator you select as an encrypted private message and may quote material you explicitly enter. Mincus Apps does not automatically receive group content or reports.

Local private keysets/group secrets are wrapped with Android Keystore. Group events and media/files use encryption, and event authors sign their updates. This implementation has not undergone an independent security audit. Members can copy, export, photograph or leak information and invitations; encryption does not prevent those actions. Do not rely on Muster for emergency delivery, safety-critical use, anonymity or guaranteed erasure.

Permissions and user choices
Nearby Devices/Bluetooth permissions allow scanning, advertising and connecting to group phones. On Android 10/11, Android requires foreground location permission for Bluetooth scanning; Muster does not call location APIs or derive physical location. Radio addresses are transient operational data and are excluded from diagnostic export. Camera permission is optional and used for invitation QR scanning; camera frames are processed locally, not saved or shared. Full manual invitation entry works without camera permission.

Photos and files are accessed only when you select them through Android pickers. Shared pictures are converted to bounded copies with source metadata removed; arbitrary library files preserve their original bytes, names/type metadata and potentially personal metadata. Review a file before sharing it. System document providers may use their own internet connections under their policies. Exporting a copy creates a file outside Muster's control.

Foreground-only sync stops when the app leaves the foreground. Optional background sync on Android 12 and newer is started by you, requests notification permission where applicable, and uses a connected-device foreground service with a Stop action. On Android 10/11, sync is foreground-only; Muster does not request background location. Background sync can exchange group metadata and eligible picture/message content while the screen is off; library file payloads remain foreground-only. Delivery depends on encounters, Bluetooth, permissions and OS power controls.

Retention and deletion
People > Settings > Leave group & erase local data removes local group records, keys and payloads after confirmation. Other members and externally saved copies remain. Your installation identity and minimal sequence high-water marks remain to prevent sequence reuse when rejoining; clear Android app storage/uninstall to remove the installation's remaining local data. Root identity recovery/device linking is not available. Android cloud/device-transfer backups are excluded. Local cache/library cleanup and conversation clearing do not remotely delete other copies; immutable encrypted relay/removal records may remain. Content inactivity limits forwarding/interactions, not automatic erasure of local history. Device storage does not guarantee forensic erasure.

Support, payments and external services
If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond. Email providers process that correspondence. Do not send invitations, private keys or unrelated group/private content. We retain correspondence as reasonably necessary to handle support and applicable records; email us to request deletion of that correspondence. Diagnostics are exported only through Android's share chooser at your request and include API level, counts and fixed error codes, excluding names, message/file contents, keys/invitations and raw radio identifiers. The chosen recipient/service handles exported data under its own policies. Google Play handles any app purchase under Google's terms. We do not sell app data or use behavioral advertising.

Children and changes
Muster has private member communication and arbitrary file sharing. It is not positioned as an app for young children; intended audience and age rating must be finalized before public release. A parent/guardian may contact support with privacy concerns. This policy does not claim Families compliance or establish an age rating. We will update the date above when practices change.
