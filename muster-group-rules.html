Muster Group Rules and Reporting

Muster Group Rules and Reporting
Version 1 - November 14, 2026

Accept these rules before creating, joining or participating in a group. Share only content and files you are entitled to share, with the consent of people whose personal information is involved.

Do not use Muster for harassment, bullying, threats, hate or discrimination; sexual exploitation or abuse (especially involving children); sexually explicit material; non-consensual intimate material; illegal activities; violence promotion; scams, impersonation, malware, or infringing material. Do not share someone else's invitation or private information without permission. Follow applicable laws and group rules. There are no paid features rewarding objectionable content.

Reporting and blocking
Open the information button beside a post, comment, poll, file, message or profile, then choose Report to a moderator. Select a known moderator and describe the issue. A report includes the item/reference and the reason you enter; it does not automatically attach the original picture, file or private-message body. Include only the evidence needed and only what you choose to disclose. Reports are queued encrypted private messages, not instant developer alerts. They need nearby encounters to reach that moderator. A delivery receipt does not mean the report has been reviewed or resolved.

Block members through People > Member actions > Block on this phone, or from the reporting dialog. Blocking hides their incoming messages/feed on this phone; it does not ban them for everyone or erase their existing copies. Moderators can archive/delete content, restore removal records and close polls using the existing signed controls. The group root can revoke devices and rotate keys. Report abusive moderators to another trusted moderator; if none is available, stop sharing, leave the group and contact mincus.apps@gmail.com. Support is an email channel and requires your chosen email app/provider; it is not a Bluetooth moderation server. Do not email invitations, keys or unnecessary private content.

Moderators should review reports at their next encounter, act on abusive material/members, and tell reporters what action they took. A decentralized group has no central monitoring service. Mincus Apps cannot remotely read, remove or recover group content. This does not guarantee protection from malicious members, immediate moderation or remote deletion. Moderation effectiveness still needs controlled multi-phone acceptance before public release.

Protect your group
Keep invitations private, verify fingerprints when identity matters, appoint a backup moderator and rotate invitations/keys after revoking an unwanted device. Someone retaining a current invitation can rejoin as a fresh identity. Members may retain/export content already received. Do not rely on delivery for emergencies or safety-critical tasks. No independently audited secure-messenger guarantee is made.

Questions, app abuse or privacy concerns: mincus.apps@gmail.com.
