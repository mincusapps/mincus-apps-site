About this policy

This policy covers the Fairy Dust Android app (com.fairydust.app), including its sand tray, treasure hunts, permanent album, and first-discovery guide.

Information on your device

Your current hunt, discovered treasures, guide progress, and preferences are stored in the app's private storage on this device. The app does not transmit these records to Mincus Apps or another service. There are no app accounts, cloud saves, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases. Its music and sound effects are bundled or generated locally.

Optional motion sensors

When tilt is enabled, the app uses available motion-sensor readings to move the sand. Readings are processed locally while the app is active and are not saved as a sensor history or transmitted. You can turn tilt off in Settings. Tilt is suspended while the first-discovery guide is active.

Payments, support, and external links

Google Play handles any purchase of the app under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. The app does not request or receive your payment-card details. If you email support, we receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use those communications to respond and troubleshoot; email providers process them to deliver that service. Please avoid sending sensitive information or children's personal information. Optional music-credit links open outside the app; the destination and your browser may process connection details under their own policies. The separate website privacy policy covers visits to our website.

Retention and deletion

Local hunt, album, guide, and settings data remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Starting a new hunt resets that hunt but keeps the permanent album. There is no cloud recovery, and progress does not automatically move between devices. The app excludes its records from Android cloud backups and device-transfer backups. Support correspondence is kept as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain applicable records. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; deleting app storage removes local progress.

Children and families

The app is intended for older children and adults. It does not request children's names, contact information, photos, location, or account details, and has no chat, user-content sharing, ads, or tracking SDKs. A parent or guardian may contact us about support correspondence or privacy concerns. We do not sell app data or use it for behavioral advertising.

Security and updates

Android app-private storage protects local records within the operating system's controls. We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the date above. Questions about this policy can be sent to mincus.apps@gmail.com.
