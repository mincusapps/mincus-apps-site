About the app

This policy covers STEAM, the Foggy Mirror Android app (com.jaredmbull.steam). It provides a touchable steamed mirror using a bundled demo reflection and an optional live camera mode.

Local drawing and settings

Drawing, fog settings and rendering state stay in the app's memory while it runs. The app does not create an account, upload your drawings, or save them as photos or files. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or cloud saves. Clearing or closing the experience can remove its temporary state.

Optional camera

The bundled demo works without camera permission or camera hardware. Only choosing Use camera requests Android camera permission. If allowed, live video frames are used locally to render your reflection. The app does not record, save, upload, share, or analyze faces in those frames. It does not request microphone, photo-library, storage or location permissions. Leaving camera mode, putting the app in the background, or closing it stops camera streams. Returning to the app leaves the bundled demo reflection active. You can revoke camera permission in Android Settings.

Network access

The Android manifest includes internet capability for its WebView host. The installed experience serves bundled content from an intercepted local HTTPS origin and blocks other resource origins and navigation. It needs no network connection to play and does not make remote requests for its mirror or camera effects. The Privacy & support entry inside Android opens this policy from bundled text. Visits to our public website are covered separately by the website privacy policy.

Payments and support

Google Play handles any purchase of the app under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. The app does not request or receive payment-card details. If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond and troubleshoot. Email providers process those communications to deliver the service. Please avoid sending sensitive information or children's personal information. We do not sell app data or use it for behavioral advertising.

Retention and deletion

The app has no server-side user account or uploaded camera/drawing history to delete. Android cloud and device-transfer backups are excluded for app records. Support correspondence is kept as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain applicable records; contact us to request deletion of that correspondence. Camera permission can be removed through Android Settings, and clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app records.

Children and privacy

The app does not request names, contact information, location, photos or account details from users, including children. Optional camera frames stay on the device as described above. A parent or guardian may contact mincus.apps@gmail.com with a privacy concern.

Updates

We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the date above. Contact mincus.apps@gmail.com with privacy questions.
