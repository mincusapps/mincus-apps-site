GAMEPLAY Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026
Developer: Mincus Apps
Contact: mincus.apps@gmail.com
Public policy: https://mincusapps.github.io/mincus-apps-site/gameplay-privacy.html

Scope

This policy covers the GAMEPLAY Android field edition (org.gameplay.fieldedition), an offline action game set in The Works. It does not cover the separate browser or original Godot editions.

On-device progress and settings

Your character, inventory, campaign progress, checkpoint backups, and preferences are stored locally in the app's WebView storage. The app does not transmit these records to Mincus Apps or another service. There are no accounts, advertising or analytics SDKs, subscriptions, in-app purchases, chat, multiplayer servers or cloud saves. Music, fonts and game content are bundled for offline use.

Permissions and connections

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, broad storage, or internet permission. Its HTTPS-looking appassets origin is intercepted locally and serves files from the installed bundle; it is not a remote game server. The native host and content security policy block remote game resource requests. Touch, optional controller or keyboard input, and audio are processed on the device.

Payments, links and support

Google Play handles any purchase of the app under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices; the app does not request or receive payment-card details. Optional HTTPS soundtrack-credit links open an external browser after a user action. The destination and browser may process connection details under their own policies. The separate website policy covers visits to our public site. If you email support, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond and troubleshoot. Email providers process communications to deliver that service. Avoid sending sensitive information or children's personal information. We do not sell app data or use it for behavioral advertising.

Retention and deletion

Progress and settings remain on this device until you use the game's reset options, clear app storage, or uninstall. Starting a new character replaces the active character after confirmation; it does not create a cloud backup. Progress does not automatically transfer between devices. App data is excluded from Android cloud and device-transfer backups. Support correspondence is kept as reasonably needed to handle requests and maintain applicable records; contact us to request deletion of that correspondence.

Children and privacy

The app does not ask users, including children, for names, contact information, photos, location or account details. A parent or guardian may contact mincus.apps@gmail.com with a privacy concern. The game's content rating and intended-audience disclosures are separate from this privacy policy.

Security and updates

Local storage is protected within Android's app storage controls. We may update this policy as the game changes and will update the date above. Contact mincus.apps@gmail.com with privacy questions.
